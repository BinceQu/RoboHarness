Complete picking_up_toys.

The grey toy box is the open grey tray on the table, on the working
edge in front of the red chair. The floor near that chair is the
working spot for the table. Do not walk along the table to the far
cabinet end.

The bed has two boxes to recover later: a green-and-white box and a
blue box. Their places on the bed change by instance. Do not assume
the blue box is at the head or that the green-and-white box is at the
foot. Judge each box from a fresh image.

The left-side corridor is the aisle on the left of the bed. The
right-side corridor is the aisle between the bed and the right wall.

First capture a fresh head image. Then do the job in this order.
Finish each numbered step before starting the next. Do not reset.

Do not activate place-object-in-container at any time.
Do not call move_tracked_point. Do not use exec_plan_pose to bypass
the grasp plan — every grasp must go through plan_grasp_point_filter_rgbd_lite
and its returned plan_id.

1. Go to the floor near the red chair at the table. Work from that
   chair-side spot. Do not start at the bed.

2. Put every toy that is already on the table or cabinet into the
   grey toy box, one at a time. Never pick up the toy box itself.
   Never take out a toy that is already inside the toy box; do not
   use an in-box toy as a tool. If a toy cannot be picked or
   dropped after a serious try, skip it and do an easier remaining
   toy first; leave the hardest for last.

   For each table-side toy, run this pick-place sequence.

   PICK

   1. Confirm both arms are free. If either arm still holds the
      previous toy, finish the drop and open the gripper before
      starting. If either arm is outstretched, call
      set_arm_to_grasp_position on that arm with gripper="open"
      (or arm="both" if both arms are out). The chassis must not
      start move_to_reach_point while an unused arm is outstretched.

   2. On the current head image, pick one visible point on the
      target toy. call move_to_reach_point on that same point.

   3. On the new head image, call
      plan_grasp_point_filter_rgbd_lite on a point on the visible
      solid body of the toy. Use plan_arm="any" first; if the
      planner returns empty or the wrist image has no red overlay,
      run this retry chain before skipping the toy:

      a. Call move_to_reach_point on another visible point on the
         same toy, then replan (plan_grasp_point_filter_rgbd_lite).
      b. If still no red overlay, call
         adjust_<chassis>_pitch_in_head_frame with pitch=-5
         (small lean-in; do not reset to -10) and replan.
      c. If still no red overlay, call move_to_reach_point again
         on the current image and replan.
      d. After the chain a–b–c, if no red overlay has appeared,
         skip this toy. Do not loop further on the same toy.

   4. Once the wrist image shows a red overlay, call exec_plan_pose
      on that plan_id. Never move the same plan by hand with
      adjust_<arm>_eef_pose_in_head_frame. The plan pose is the
      only thing that may carry the arm into pre-grasp; do not
      hand-drive the arm to that pose.

   5. Call close_gripper on that arm. The close_gripper return
      value and any gap you can see in the wrist view do not by
      themselves prove failure — the simulator has noise on both.
      The only real grasp check is step 6.

   6. Call adjust_<holding_arm>_eef_pose_in_head_frame on the
      holding arm with z=+0.2 only (robot-base up; no x or y).
      Then inspect the head and wrist images:
        - If the toy clearly rises with the gripper, the grasp
          held. Go to PLACE.
        - If the toy is still on the surface, the grasp failed.
          Call set_arm_to_grasp_position on that arm with
          gripper="open", recapture, and go back to step 3 to
          replan on this toy. Do not try the same plan_id twice.
          If the toy has moved since the original move_to_reach_point
          in step 2, do not replan from the old reach pose; first
          go back to step 2 and call move_to_reach_point on the
          toy again before replanning.

   PLACE

   1. On the current head image, pick one visible point on the grey
      toy box itself (the tray, not the chair, not the floor, not
      the cabinet). If that toy box is off to one side, call
      spin_to_facing_point on it, then use the new image.
      Immediately call move_to_reach_point on a visible point on
      that same toy box.

   2. On the new head image, call track_object_distance on one
      interior point of the grey toy box. Name it toybox_center.
      Click inside the opening, not the floor, not the chair.
      Read that point's depth_m.

   3. If depth_m is greater than 0.7, do not start the two-point
      track yet. Look at the current image: is something between
      you and the toy box keeping you from getting closer (desk
      edge, chair, cabinet, bed, or a jammed footprint)? If yes,
      go around that blocker and find a clear way to approach the
      toy box. Hint: the floor near the red chair is the easier
      approach. Do not go to the far cabinet end of the table.
      Then, if the toy box is off to one side,
      spin_to_facing_point on it; immediately call
      move_to_reach_point on the toy box again; re-track one
      interior point as toybox_center; read depth_m again. Repeat
      this adjust until depth_m is 0.7 or less.

   4. When depth_m is 0.7 or less, call track_object_distance once
      with exactly these two points:
        toybox_center — visible interior of the grey toy box opening
        held_toy — visible solid body of the object in the gripper
      Both clicks must be on those surfaces and inside the image.
      Do not click the floor. Do not track a finger tip.

   5. After that track is live, read both current
      xyz_in_robot_base_coord_m from persistent_tracking. Compute
        dx = held_toy.x - toybox_center.x
        dy = held_toy.y - toybox_center.y
        held_z and box_z from those same tracks.
      Lift first. While held_z is not greater than box_z + 0.25,
      call adjust_left_eef_pose_in_head_frame or
      adjust_right_eef_pose_in_head_frame on the holding arm with
      only z (robot-base up). Do not send x or y on those lift
      calls. After each lift, re-read both tracks. If a point is
      lost, recapture and rebind both points before the next
      adjust.

   6. Only after held_z > box_z + 0.25, adjust XY. From the latest
      tracks, compute the held-to-center XY difference:
        x = toybox_center.x - held_toy.x
        y = toybox_center.y - held_toy.y
      Call adjust_left_eef_pose_in_head_frame or
      adjust_right_eef_pose_in_head_frame on the holding arm with
      those x and y so held_toy moves onto toybox_center. Do not
      lower z while aligning XY. After each move, re-read both
      tracks and recompute dx, dy, x, y, held_z, box_z. If held_z
      falls to box_z + 0.25 or below, lift again before any more XY.

   7. When |dx| < 0.05 and |dy| < 0.05 and held_z > box_z + 0.25,
      capture a fresh head image and inspect it carefully: is the
      held object visibly directly above the grey toy box opening?
      If yes, open_gripper on the holding arm. Immediately after
      that open_gripper, call adjust_<holding_arm>_eef_pose_in_head_frame
      on the SAME holding arm with z=+0.2 (robot-base up) and x=0,
      y=0. Do not send x or y on that lift call. This lift keeps
      the gripper and wrist clear of the toy box rim while you
      withdraw, so the toy box does not get knocked over. Do not
      lower the object by hand after that. This is the only allowed
      open_gripper for that toy. The gripper stays closed through
      every adjust. If the image shows the object is not over the
      opening, do not open. Recapture, rebind both toybox_center
      and held_toy, and go back to PLACE step 5.

   8. After the withdraw lift, call set_arm_to_grasp_position on
      the placing arm with gripper="open" (or arm="both" if the
      other arm is also out) before moving to the next toy.

3. Look at the bed. If a bed box is already gone, skip it. For each
   box still on the bed, decide from a fresh head image which of
   these three is nearer that box:
     - the foot of the bed
     - the left-side corridor
     - the right-side corridor
   Rank the remaining boxes by that nearness:
     - nearer the foot: easiest. Call move_to_reach_point on the
       box, then pick it with PICK steps 1–6 above. Do not enter a
       corridor.
     - nearer the left-side corridor: medium. Enter the left-side
       corridor and pick it there with PICK steps 1–6.
     - nearer the right-side corridor: hardest. Use the sideways
       right-corridor pickup at the end of this prompt. This case
       is uncommon.
   Pick the easier remaining box first. After the grasp is
   confirmed by PICK step 6, run PLACE steps 1–8 on it. Do the
   hardest remaining box last. If a box cannot be picked or
   dropped after a serious try, skip it and finish the easier
   remaining work first. After each drop, re-check the other box
   on a fresh image before going to it.

4. Keep working until the robot has physically returned to its spawn
   point. Only that counts as task completion. Do not stop early and
   do not call reset before that moment. Every additional toy
   placed in the toy box scores a point — keep trying as long as
   the session is alive. If no bed boxes remain, stay at the toy
   box and look for any toy you can still reach. If a toy is out
   of reach but not yet attempted, try the reach-replan chain in
   PICK steps 2–3 before giving up on it.

Sideways right-corridor pickup
Use this only if a bed box is nearer the right-side corridor than
the foot or the left-side corridor. Most instances will not need
it. Do not use it for table toys, a foot-side box, or a
left-corridor box.

S1. Activate traverse-narrow-passages and enter the right-side
    corridor sideways. That target is only reachable after you pass
    through this corridor; do not treat a nearer box as this target.

S2. Stay in the corridor. Run PICK steps 1–6 on that target box.
    If the planner says too far, keep adjusting in the corridor
    instead of leaving. Leaving empty-handed does not finish this
    pickup.

S3. Leave by the same corridor. Keep the box in the gripper.
    Then drop it with PLACE steps 1–8.

Activate at most one skill at a time. The only skills this task may
activate are traverse-narrow-passages and pick-up-object.

==== REMINDER ====

Keep working until the robot has physically returned to its spawn
point — only then is the task finished. Do not stop early and do
not finish before that moment. Every additional toy placed in the
toy box scores a point, so keep trying as long as the session is
alive. Place every visible toy; one more in the box is one more
point.
